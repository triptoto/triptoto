Browser storage

Cookies & local storage

Tripto uses a small amount of browser storage to make the app secure, reliable and useful offline. We do not use advertising cookies.

Last updated 16 September 2026tripto.to

What we use

Essential sign-in cookiesShort-lived security cookies may be used during the Google sign-in handoff. They help complete authentication safely.
Local storageYour browser may store your chosen theme, current trip, offline cache and pending changes so the app works between visits.
Service worker cacheThe app shell and selected travel data can be cached on-device for quicker and offline access.
Checkout providerIf you open a Plus checkout or billing portal, Lemon Squeezy may use its own cookies under its privacy terms.

What we do not use

Tripto does not set advertising cookies or sell cookie-derived information. We do not use your itinerary to build an advertising profile.

How to control it

You can clear Tripto’s local data from Account, or clear site data in your browser settings. Clearing browser storage can sign you out, remove locally saved documents and discard unsynced changes on that device.

A practical noteBrowser storage helps Tripto work offline. If you use a shared device, sign out after use and avoid saving sensitive files locally.
