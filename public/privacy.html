Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

tripto.to turns your travel bookings into a single, calm timeline. This policy explains what we store, why, and the control you have over it.

What we store

  • Trip data you add — trips, bookings, dates, and notes you create or forward to us.
  • Account identity — if you sign in with Google, your verified email address and Google account identifier, used only to attach your trips to your account.
  • Documents you save offline — kept on your own device. File contents are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use it

  • To build and sync your timeline across your devices.
  • To detect changes and conflicts so nothing is silently overwritten.
  • To keep your account secure and signed in.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your itinerary content for advertising.

Forwarded booking emails

When you forward a confirmation to go@tripto.to, we parse the details in memory to create timeline entries. The raw email body is not stored after parsing.

Diagnostics

Support bundles and internal metrics contain only coarse counts, internal IDs, and timestamps. They never include confirmation numbers, invite tokens, email bodies, addresses, traveler names, or document file bytes.

Your controls

  • Sign out at any time from the Account screen. Local documents stay on your phone.
  • Request deletion of your account data — contact us and we remove your trips and devices.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email go@tripto.to.
