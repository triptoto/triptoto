Your data, your trip

Privacy, clearly explained

Tripto is designed to help you organise a trip without turning your travel plans into advertising data. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated 16 September 2026tripto.to

What we collect

Account informationYour verified Google email address and account identifier when you choose to sign in.
Trip informationTrips, bookings, dates, notes and shared-trip details you choose to add.
Service dataLimited technical records needed to keep the service secure, reliable and in sync.
On-device documentsFiles you save for offline use stay on your device; their contents are not uploaded to Tripto servers.

How we use it

  • To show, sync and protect the trips you create.
  • To process booking details that you explicitly add or forward for review.
  • To prevent accidental overwrites and maintain the security of your account.
  • To respond when you request support or account deletion.

We do not sell itinerary content or use it to target advertising.

Booking emails and imports

When you forward a confirmation to go@tripto.to, Tripto processes it to identify travel details for your review. Smart Import processes supported files on your device; raw files and extracted text are not uploaded through that flow.

Your choices

  • You can sign out from Account at any time.
  • You can remove local files and cached trip data from the device that stores them.
  • You can delete your account in Account → Privacy & data, or request deletion by email. See Delete your account.
Privacy by designSupport diagnostics use coarse operational information. They exclude confirmation numbers, invite tokens, email bodies, traveller names, addresses and document file bytes.
