Account deletion

Delete your tripto.to account

You can delete your account and the trip data stored on our servers at any time, from the app or by email.

Last updated 30 September 2026tripto.to

Delete in the app

  1. Open tripto.to (web or Android app) and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Account, then Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. Guest users without an account can use Remove local data on the same screen.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address of your Google account with the subject “Delete my account”. We verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days. We never ask for passwords.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sign-in identities, devices and sessions.
  • Trips you own, with their bookings, travelers, contacts, notes, checklists, invites and sync history.
  • Your membership in trips shared with you. Trips owned by other people stay with their owners.
  • Forwarded booking emails, verified sender addresses and Plus entitlement records.

What is kept

  • An anonymous deletion record with counts only (number of trips and devices), without your name, email or trip content.
  • Billing records that Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, must keep under tax law. Cancel an active Plus subscription in the billing portal before deleting your account; deleting the account does not cancel the subscription.

Data on your phone

Documents, saved spots and cached trips stored only on your phone are removed when you delete the account in the app. On Android you can also clear them by uninstalling the app.

Deletion request

Send the request from the email address linked to your account.
