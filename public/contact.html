Include only what is needed

Tell us what screen you were on, what you expected and what happened. Do not include passport details, full payment-card information, booking confirmation numbers or document files unless we specifically ask through a secure channel.

Response and billing

Tripto support is handled by email. Subscription management and cancellation are handled in the Lemon Squeezy billing portal linked from Account for paid Plus users.
