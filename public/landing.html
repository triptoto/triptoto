BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A few things
you might wonder.

No grand promises.
Just a clearer way to travel.

What can I keep in Tripto?+

Bring flights, stays, transport, activities, and saved places into one trip. Keep the details alongside a day-by-day plan, so you can see how everything fits together.

How do I add my bookings?+

Add bookings yourself, or use the booking import flow to review supported confirmations before saving. Check the dates, times, and places in the review step; you stay in control of the details.

Can I plan a trip with someone else?+

Yes. Sign in to use Tripto’s collaboration features and invite the people travelling with you. Access depends on the sharing permissions for that trip.

Does Tripto book flights or hotels for me?+

Tripto organizes your travel plans. Your reservations stay with the airline, hotel, or travel provider you booked with.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes. Tripto is built for a mobile browser, and you can add it to your home screen for quick access. You can also open your trips on a larger screen.

Will it show live flight updates?+

Live flight monitoring is not currently enabled. Use your airline or airport for the latest departure, gate, and delay information.
